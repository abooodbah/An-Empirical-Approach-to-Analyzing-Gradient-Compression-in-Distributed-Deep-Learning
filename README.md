# Measurement artifact: An Empirical Systems Analysis of Gradient Compression in Distributed Deep Learning

This repository holds the measurement harness, raw per-rank records,
launch scripts, and processing data behind the paper "An Empirical
Systems Analysis of Gradient Compression in Distributed Deep Learning"
(earlier drafts carried the working title "Beyond Compression Ratio";
the repository name keeps it). Nothing here is post-processed beyond the CSV
aggregations in `processed/`; every number in the paper can be traced
to a per-rank record in `raw/`.

The study asks one question: when gradient compression fails to reduce
wall-clock step time, where did the cost go? The harness answers it by
timing every phase of every training step under device synchronization,
on every rank, and by calibrating the collectives directly on the same
allocations.

## Layout

- `harness/`: the benchmark programs and launch scripts.
  `paper_workload_benchmark.py` is the frozen July harness that
  produced the main campaign. `control_workload_benchmark.py` is the
  September control variant, generated from the frozen file by
  `make_control_harness.py`; it adds a shared-scale summable int8 mode
  exchanged by all-reduce, a Top-k mode with standard local error
  feedback, and an uninstrumented wall-clock timing mode. The frozen
  file is unchanged; the generator asserts every edit.
  `control_workload_benchmark_v3.py`, generated in turn from the
  September control file by `make_control_harness_v3.py`, adds a
  LLaMA-style decoder workload of about 1.05e9 parameters (18 layers,
  d 2048, 16 heads, SwiGLU 5504, vocabulary 32000, sequence 2048) and two
  production overlap modes: PyTorch's fp16 compression hook and its
  per-tensor int8 hook, the latter ported with device-agnostic tensor
  placement so the same algorithm runs on the XPU track. Each generator
  leaves its source unchanged and asserts every edit.
- `contract/`: the experiment contract and campaign protocol the July
  runs were validated against.
- `raw/july/`: the July campaign archives: three independent H100 2x4
  seed jobs (3328658 to 3328660, host-staged NDR InfiniBand) and the
  four-node Intel weak-scaling job (3323561), with checksums. Each
  archive holds per-rank CSV, JSONL, and provenance records for every
  condition, plus transport validations.
- `raw/control/`: the September control campaign: the reducible int8
  and local-error-feedback conditions, uninstrumented timing repeats,
  and second-allocation Intel endpoint repeats.
- `raw/round4/`: the Lonestar6 A100 campaign behind Section VII and
  the lower block of Table VII: pilot and 12-rank sweep (jobs 3459209,
  3459786), 24-rank sweep (3459984), synchronization-interval sweep
  (3460161), 12-rank intervention pairs in a second allocation
  (3460182), and the 1B PowerSGD rerun on Stampede3 (3523891). Every
  run used `control_workload_benchmark_v4.py` (generated by
  `make_control_harness_v4.py`, which adds the exact-sum all-gather arm,
  FP16 dense, bit-equality verification, and the barrier-free
  synchronization-interval mode) through `harness/launch/ls6_r4.slurm`.
- `diagnostic/`: the frozen diagnostic procedure and its held-out
  evaluation (below).
- `processed/`: the aggregated CSVs the figures and tables read.
- `figures/`: the scripts that regenerate every figure from `raw/`
  and `processed/`.
- `environment/`: software identity per platform track.

## Diagnostic procedure and held-out evaluation (Section VII)

`diagnostic/rules.py` is the rule file the paper calls frozen: version
`v1-2026-09-21`, sha256 `71329c336cc3e964...` (full digest and freeze
time in `diagnostic/RULES_FROZEN.sha256`). Its thresholds were set from
the Stampede3 records (tags A, B, C, V; features in
`features_dev.json`) before any Lonestar6 round-4 run was submitted, and
no Lonestar6 record was used to set them. `E6_PROTOCOL.md` is the
pre-registered protocol: the twelve cases, the paired measurements each
needs, the scoring rule, and the job ledger, with a dated interim
record written after J3 and a dated reporting addendum written after
the final scoring. Nothing in the protocol or the rules was changed
after the fact; the addendum explains two label changes.

To regenerate Table VIII from the raw records:

```
tar -xzf raw/round4/*.tgz -C <workdir>
python diagnostic/features.py <workdir> "" features_r4.json
python diagnostic/score_e6.py features_r4.json
```

The first command extracts the per-rank CSVs; the second computes the
instrumented features (exchange, codec and barrier shares, the
calibrated shape ratio, the received-traffic ratio) for every
condition, one row per model, mode, rank count, and allocation; the
third applies the frozen rules to each case's instrumented record and
scores the named intervention against the two uninstrumented steps
from the same allocation. The output is `E6_FINAL.txt`: the per-case
table first, counts second, then the three ablations scored the same
way. `alloc_compare.py` reproduces the between-allocation comparison
cited in VII-B, and `peak_mem_1b.py` the device-memory arithmetic of
Section VIII.

Outcome labels: correct (delta at least the larger of 0.05 and half
the predicted effect), short (delta at least 0.05 but under half the
prediction), null (|delta| under 0.05), harmful (delta at or below
-0.05); abstentions are scored by whether any tried intervention paid.
The family label on each row records what the intervention changed:
only cases 3 and 4 (exact-sum all-gather against all-reduce on
identical encoded inputs, arms verified bit-equal) change the
collective alone; every other intervention is a substitution of one
implementation for another.

## Measurement conventions

Two conventions matter when reading the raw records.

1. Phase timing is exposed cost. Every phase boundary is device
   synchronized and an explicit barrier separates iterations, so
   phases are disjoint and attributable. This deliberately suppresses
   overlap; the DDP control bounds what overlap recovers, and the
   wall-mode records in `raw/control/` measure the same conditions
   with no instrumentation beyond one synchronization per step.
2. Step time is the per-iteration maximum across ranks. Phase
   quantities pool rank-iterations. Percentiles are computed within a
   job and averaged across jobs where independent jobs exist.

## Reproducing

Each run directory under the raw archives is self-describing: the
provenance JSON records the command line, source hash, library
versions, Slurm layout, and calibration constants for that run. The
launch scripts under `harness/launch/` are the exact scripts the
campaigns used; they expect the TACC Stampede3 environment described
in `environment/versions.md`.

The collective cost model in the paper (Section IV) is fitted from
`processed/h100_fabric_collectives.csv`; the weak-scaling table from
`processed/intel_step_scaling.csv`; the phase attribution from the
per-rank records of the three H100 seeds, jobs 3328658, 3328659, and
3328660. `raw/july/h100-3328657.tar.gz` is a qualification job with
six conditions and is not part of the figure; including it moves the
Transformer's CPU-staged step from 240.3 to 247.7 ms. To rebuild the
phase-attribution figure and its numbers, extract the three seed
archives into one folder and point `TPDS_RAW_SEEDS` at it before
running `figures/make_figures.py`.

## Validation campaign (v2)

`validation_v2/` holds the September 2026 measurement-validity campaign:
the frozen analysis rules (`ANALYSIS_RULES_v2.md`, hash in
`RULES_v2.sha256`), the v5 harness with its manifests and run lists, and
the frozen analysis code (`analysis/analyze_v2.py`, hashes in
`CODE_v2.sha256`). The job records are under `raw/v2/`, one archive per
Lonestar6 job: SMOKE 3465525; V1, V2, and V3 3465526 to 3465528, three
independent four-node allocations repeating every condition at 12 ranks;
L1 3465529, the 1B workload at two nodes; and the follow-up R1 3471881.
Analysis outputs are under `validation_v2/results/` (2026-09-26 covers
the five original jobs; 2026-09-29 adds R1).

Every ResNet-50 run in V1 to V3 fails the frozen validity gate because
the v5 harness hashed the full model state, which includes BatchNorm
running buffers, and those buffers legitimately differ across ranks
under per-rank inputs. The follow-up job R1 reran the 32 ResNet-50
conditions with harness v5.1
(`harness/control_workload_benchmark_v5_1.py`, generated by
`harness/build_r1.py`), which also records a parameters-only hash, the
quantity the rules name; the parameters were equal on every R1 run.
`analysis/analyze_r1.py` wraps the frozen `analyze_v2.py`, reading the
parameters-only hash for R1 records and adding R1 to the job list; the
V1 to V3 and L1 rows are unchanged by the wrapper. One R1 condition,
`A-resnet50-gpu-topk-S`, lost one of its two passes to a rendezvous
connection failure at launch and is reported from its single pass; the
job log and per-condition logs are inside the R1 archive. Follow-up
code hashes are in `validation_v2/CODE_R1.sha256`.

## Corrections and provenance

`diagnostic/v1_1/` holds a retrospective audit of the E6 evaluation
(2026-09-29): five corrections applied to copies of the frozen rows
(`CORRECTIONS.md`), the counts under every reading (`E6_CORRECTED.txt`),
and the record of the feature extractor's changes after the freeze
(`EXTRACTOR_FREEZE.md`). The frozen files are unchanged and
`diagnostic/v1_1/test_frozen_regen.py` regenerates `E6_FINAL.txt` from
them. `provenance/PROVENANCE.md` gives the chronology with GitHub push
times and Slurm submission times. It also records that every E6 job ran
harness v4 with sha256 3aecaae1 (git 35cf9be); the current
`harness/control_workload_benchmark_v4.py` was edited afterward
(ef3ed2d), so a rerun of E6 should check out that file from 35cf9be.
The revision's experiments were pre-registered before submission:
E7 in `diagnostic/E7_PROTOCOL.md` and E8 in `diagnostic/E8_PROTOCOL.md`;
`provenance/rev_jobs.tsv` lists their jobs.

## Boundary

These are execution-cost measurements on synthetic fixed-input
workloads. No accuracy or convergence claim is supported, and the
multi-node transports are host-staged; the records include the
transport validations that prove which path ran.
